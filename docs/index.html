Privacy Policy

Maker Business Command Center

Effective date: November 24, 2026

This policy explains how the Maker Business Command Center developer handles information when you use the Maker Business Command Center app (the “App”) or visit this policy page. The current App is designed to keep business records on your device; the developer does not operate an account service or cloud-sync service for App data.

Information you enter in the App

You may enter business records such as customer names and contact details, invoice and payment amounts, expense and vendor details, inventory and supplier information, tasks, notes, and locally created usernames and roles. The App also saves preferences and a local change history. Only enter personal information that you are authorized to use.

How the App uses and stores information

The App uses this information to provide its customer, sales, expense, inventory, task, reporting, and account features. In the current version, records and local account data are stored in the App's private storage on your device. The App does not send those records to a developer-operated server, and it does not include developer-operated advertising or analytics services. Local account passcodes are stored as salted, derived hashes rather than as plaintext passcodes.

Importing, exporting, and sharing

You can import spreadsheets and export reports or business records. These files may contain personal or confidential business information. When you choose to export, share, or save a file, the operating system and any destination you select may process it under their own privacy terms. Keep exported files secure; deleting data from the App does not delete copies you exported or shared.

Disclosure and sale

The developer does not receive or sell the business records stored in the App. The developer does not disclose App records to third parties from the App. This does not cover information you choose to share using your device or other services, which are governed by those services' terms and privacy policies.

Retention and deletion

Your records remain in the App's local storage until you delete them or reset the App. An administrator can use the App's data purge or factory reset features to remove selected operational records or all App data, accounts, history, and preferences from the device. Factory reset does not remove copies stored outside the App, including exported files or backups. Device operating-system backup settings may also affect whether App data is included in a device backup.

Security

The App uses device-private storage and stores local passcodes as salted, derived hashes. No method of storage is completely secure. Protect access to your device and any exported files, and avoid entering payment-card numbers, bank login credentials, or other highly sensitive information into the App.

Children

The App is intended for business operations and is not directed to children. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the App.

This policy page

This static page is hosted by GitHub Pages. The page itself does not use advertising, analytics, or tracking scripts. GitHub may process technical information when it serves this page; see the GitHub Privacy Statement for information about GitHub's handling of visitor data.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the App's features or data practices change. The effective date above indicates when this version was last updated. Please review this page periodically.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests concerning data stored in the App, email the Maker Business Command Center developer at SmilesCreativeOutlet@gmail.com. Because the developer does not receive locally stored App data, deletion of that data must be performed in the App on the device where it is stored.
